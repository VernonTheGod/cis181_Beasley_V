Fish, wings, lemon pepper

Shark's Fish & Chicken

Shark's is the newer stop in this argument. By the early 2000s it was already a known Chicago franchise, with fish on the menu next to the chicken. This page does not invent a founder story, because the public accounts do not line up the way they do for Harold's and Uncle Remus.

How it is different

Harold's and Uncle Remus are tied to mild sauce. Shark's is the place people name for lemon pepper. A usual first order is lemon-pepper wings, or a fish dinner such as catfish, with fries. The restaurants also sell shrimp and other fried fish, so it is a fish-and-chicken spot, not only a chicken shack.

Locations spread across the South Side, the West Side, and nearby suburbs. The food is the point of this page, not a live map of every address.

Where it fits

If someone wants the oldest story, send them to Harold's. If they want the West Side mild-sauce wings, send them to Uncle Remus. If they want lemon pepper or fish with the chicken, Shark's is the one that matches.
