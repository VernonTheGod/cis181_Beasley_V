A guide, not an order form

Three spots. One Chicago argument.

Harold's, Uncle Remus, and Shark's are the names people say when they talk about the best chicken in the city. This site explains where each one came from and what to order the first time.

Compare them

Since 1950

Harold's

South Side chicken over fries and white bread, with mild or hot sauce. Ask for it fried hard.

Since 1969

Uncle Remus

West Side wings and a mild sauce built by the Rickette family. Still a family business.
